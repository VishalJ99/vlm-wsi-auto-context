Audit this text review of candidate crops.

Input:
- consensus: the shared appearance of the main crop group
- crops: per-crop label and reason
- flagged_artifacts: crops already removed by the review

Task:
Find kept crops whose reason suggests they were accepted only because a small
or incidental part matched the consensus.

Mark a kept crop as "suspect_false_keep" if its reason uses language like:
- small fragment
- tiny region
- small corner/edge/peripheral area
- contains some matching tissue
- faint/low-contrast fragment
and does not clearly say the main region of the crop matches the consensus.

Do not mark a kept crop suspect if the reason says the crop shows the main
consensus pattern, a large tissue core, clear shared architecture, or is
broadly consistent with the consensus.

Do not change crops already listed in flagged_artifacts unless the text is
internally contradictory.

Use only the written reasons. Do not infer visual facts that are not stated.
