- Completed usable non-SV40 cases available for sampling were EVG `100`, H&E
  `100`, JONES `100`, and PAS `60`. The reduced PAS count reflects the already
  identified invalid PAS path rows in this scale-up worklist, not a detector
  failure.
- The Stage 1 giant-box artifact was caused by padding raw boxes by `25%` before
  overlap-over-smaller merging. A long artifact-like raw box can become a bridge
  after padding, causing transitive hull merging across otherwise separate tissue
  candidates.

Interpretation:

distillation because downstream detector labels are based on raw Stage 1 boxes
and later filtered candidates, not the legacy Stage 1 padded/merged overlay.
Future optimization could add conservative early duplicate suppression on raw
boxes to reduce redundant VLM crop calls: merge/suppress only high-confidence
duplicates, without pre-merge padding, for example high IoU or near-containment
with a bounded larger/smaller area ratio. Final display/training-box padding
should remain a late post-filtering operation.

## 2026-06-01 - PER-248 Scale-500 YOLO Distillation Readout

Objective:
Distill the scale-500 VLM detector labels into a high-recall thumbnail detector
and determine whether coarse YOLO tuning materially changes recall.

Method:
- Exported the completed non-SV40 and SV40-skip-odd-one-out detector outputs as a
  supervised COCO/YOLO dataset with 500 thumbnails and 2,167 weak-label boxes.
- Trained five coarse Ultralytics YOLO variants on the exported dataset:
  YOLO11n default, reduced augmentation, stain-jitter augmentation, AdamW cosine
  schedule, and YOLO11s default.
- Evaluated on the held-out 50-case test split with strict IoU>=0.50 matching,
  low-confidence threshold sweeps, and a corrected recall-first coverage audit
  using `IoU>=0.50 OR intersection/GT_area>=0.90`.
- Asked a subagent to inspect the strict-IoU missed GT boxes qualitatively,
  treating the exported VLM boxes as weak labels rather than pathologist truth.

Outputs:
- Dataset export:
  `/data2/vj724/vlm-wsi-auto-context/runs/detector_training_datasets/scale500_per248_coco_yolo_v1/`
- YOLO run root:
  `/data2/vj724/vlm-wsi-auto-context/runs/detector_distillation/yolo_scale500_per248_v1/`
- Report:
  `/data2/vj724/vlm-wsi-auto-context/runs/detector_distillation/yolo_scale500_per248_v1/scale500_yolo_distillation_report_v1/report.md`
- Corrected coverage audit PDF:
  `/data2/vj724/vlm-wsi-auto-context/runs/detector_distillation/yolo_scale500_per248_v1/gt_coverage_audit_yolo11n_stainjitter_conf005_v1/gt_coverage_audit.pdf`
- Qualitative missed-GT audit:
  `/data2/vj724/vlm-wsi-auto-context/runs/detector_distillation/yolo_scale500_per248_v1/missed_gt_audit_subagent/audit_summary.md`

Observations:
- The best recall-first YOLO run was `yolo11n_img1024_e60_stainjitter`.
- At `conf=0.05`, strict IoU>=0.50 recall was `201/213 = 94.4%`, but the
  corrected coverage-aware recall was `211/213 = 99.1%`.
- Lowering the same model to `conf=0.01` gave coverage-aware recall
  `213/213 = 100%` on the test split, at the expected cost of more false
  positives.
- The apparent strict-IoU misses were mostly broader YOLO detections containing
  smaller or fragmented weak-label boxes. The subagent audit categorized nine as
  likely weak-label granularity/non-actionable, two as ambiguous, and one as a
  useful true YOLO miss at `conf=0.05`; that true miss is recovered by lowering
  the threshold to about `0.04`/`0.01`.

Interpretation:
For this recall-first detector, strict IoU is a localization/tightness metric,
not the main tissue-recall metric. Coverage over GT is the right safety metric
for deciding whether downstream tissue is available to the review loop. The
current practical route is `yolo11n_img1024_e60_stainjitter`, using `conf=0.05`
when review budget matters and `conf=0.01` when maximizing recall is more
important than false-positive load.

## 2026-06-01 - PER-249 Stage 6 Tissue Linear Probe

Objective:
Train a lightweight tissue/artifact classifier from the scale-500 detector
pipeline Stage 5/6 intermediate outputs, using all Stage 6 rejected crops and a
balanced accepted set matched across stains.

Method:
- Loaded Stage 6 classification rows from the scale-500 non-SV40 and
  SV40-skip-odd-one-out detector runs.
- Used all 51 Stage 6 `no` crops as artifact/rejected examples.
- Sampled 51 Stage 6 `yes` crops as accepted examples, preserving stain balance;
  41 accepted examples were matched from the same case and 10 used same-stain
  fallback matches.
- Extracted frozen pretrained DINOv2-small (`vit_small_patch14_dinov2`) features
  and trained `StandardScaler + LogisticRegression(liblinear)`.
- Ran the same protocol on two image sources: high-resolution Stage 5 WSI reread
  crops and thumbnail-derived bbox crops.
- Applied each fitted probe to the remaining 2,194 accepted crops at artifact
  probability threshold `0.5` and generated proposed-rejection PDFs.

Outputs:
- High-resolution run:
  `/data2/vj724/vlm-wsi-auto-context/runs/stage6_tissue_classifier/scale500_dinov2small_linear_probe_highres_v1/`
- High-resolution proposed-rejection PDF:
  `/data2/vj724/vlm-wsi-auto-context/runs/stage6_tissue_classifier/scale500_dinov2small_linear_probe_highres_v1/visuals/proposed_rejections.pdf`
- Thumbnail-bbox run:
  `/data2/vj724/vlm-wsi-auto-context/runs/stage6_tissue_classifier/scale500_dinov2small_linear_probe_thumbnail_bbox_v1/`
- Thumbnail-bbox proposed-rejection PDF:
  `/data2/vj724/vlm-wsi-auto-context/runs/stage6_tissue_classifier/scale500_dinov2small_linear_probe_thumbnail_bbox_v1/visuals/proposed_rejections.pdf`

Observations:
- Available labeled crop inventory was 2,296 crops: 2,245 accepted and 51
  rejected. Rejections by stain were EVG 18, H&E 2, JONES 12, PAS 4, and SV40
  15.
- High-resolution crop CV: accuracy `0.912`, ROC AUC `0.968`, AP `0.968`,
  confusion `TN=46 FP=5 FN=4 TP=47`.
- Thumbnail-bbox crop CV: accuracy `0.853`, ROC AUC `0.959`, AP `0.958`,
  confusion `TN=42 FP=9 FN=6 TP=45`.
- Applied to the remaining accepted crops, the high-resolution run proposed
  260 rejections (`11.85%`) and the thumbnail-bbox run proposed 339
  (`15.45%`). The proposed lists overlapped on 191 crops, with 69
  high-resolution-only and 148 thumbnail-only candidates.

Interpretation:
The high-resolution Stage 5 WSI rereads appear to help. Both image sources have
signal, but the thumbnail run proposes substantially more rejections and has
lower cross-validation accuracy, consistent with a noisier view of the same
candidate boxes. Use the high-resolution run as the primary readout and the
thumbnail run as a secondary candidate-mining/audit list unless pathologist
review shows otherwise.
