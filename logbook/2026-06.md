Observations:
- Completed usable non-SV40 cases available for sampling were EVG `100`, H&E
  `100`, JONES `100`, and PAS `60`. The reduced PAS count reflects the already
  identified invalid PAS path rows in this scale-up worklist, not a detector
  failure.
- The Stage 1 giant-box artifact was caused by padding raw boxes by `25%` before
  overlap-over-smaller merging. A long artifact-like raw box can become a bridge
  after padding, causing transitive hull merging across otherwise separate tissue
  candidates.

Interpretation:
The Stage 1 postprocessed overlay artifact is not a current blocker for
distillation because downstream detector labels are based on raw Stage 1 boxes
and later filtered candidates, not the legacy Stage 1 padded/merged overlay.
Future optimization could add conservative early duplicate suppression on raw
boxes to reduce redundant VLM crop calls: merge/suppress only high-confidence
duplicates, without pre-merge padding, for example high IoU or near-containment
with a bounded larger/smaller area ratio. Final display/training-box padding
should remain a late post-filtering operation.
