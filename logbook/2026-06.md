## 2026-06-01 - PER-248 Scale-500 YOLO Distillation Readout

Objective:
Distill the scale-500 VLM detector labels into a high-recall thumbnail detector
and determine whether coarse YOLO tuning materially changes recall.

Method:
- Exported the completed non-SV40 and SV40-skip-odd-one-out detector outputs as a
  supervised COCO/YOLO dataset with 500 thumbnails and 2,167 weak-label boxes.
- Trained five coarse Ultralytics YOLO variants on the exported dataset:
  YOLO11n default, reduced augmentation, stain-jitter augmentation, AdamW cosine
  schedule, and YOLO11s default.
- Evaluated on the held-out 50-case test split with strict IoU>=0.50 matching,
  low-confidence threshold sweeps, and a corrected recall-first coverage audit
  using `IoU>=0.50 OR intersection/GT_area>=0.90`.
- Asked a subagent to inspect the strict-IoU missed GT boxes qualitatively,
  treating the exported VLM boxes as weak labels rather than pathologist truth.

Outputs:
- Dataset export:
  `/data2/vj724/vlm-wsi-auto-context/runs/detector_training_datasets/scale500_per248_coco_yolo_v1/`
- YOLO run root:
  `/data2/vj724/vlm-wsi-auto-context/runs/detector_distillation/yolo_scale500_per248_v1/`
- Report:
  `/data2/vj724/vlm-wsi-auto-context/runs/detector_distillation/yolo_scale500_per248_v1/scale500_yolo_distillation_report_v1/report.md`
- Corrected coverage audit PDF:
  `/data2/vj724/vlm-wsi-auto-context/runs/detector_distillation/yolo_scale500_per248_v1/gt_coverage_audit_yolo11n_stainjitter_conf005_v1/gt_coverage_audit.pdf`
- Qualitative missed-GT audit:
  `/data2/vj724/vlm-wsi-auto-context/runs/detector_distillation/yolo_scale500_per248_v1/missed_gt_audit_subagent/audit_summary.md`

Observations:
- The best recall-first YOLO run was `yolo11n_img1024_e60_stainjitter`.
- At `conf=0.05`, strict IoU>=0.50 recall was `201/213 = 94.4%`, but the
  corrected coverage-aware recall was `211/213 = 99.1%`.
- Lowering the same model to `conf=0.01` gave coverage-aware recall
  `213/213 = 100%` on the test split, at the expected cost of more false
  positives.
- The apparent strict-IoU misses were mostly broader YOLO detections containing
  smaller or fragmented weak-label boxes. The subagent audit categorized nine as
  likely weak-label granularity/non-actionable, two as ambiguous, and one as a
  useful true YOLO miss at `conf=0.05`; that true miss is recovered by lowering
  the threshold to about `0.04`/`0.01`.

Interpretation:
For this recall-first detector, strict IoU is a localization/tightness metric,
not the main tissue-recall metric. Coverage over GT is the right safety metric
for deciding whether downstream tissue is available to the review loop. The
current practical route is `yolo11n_img1024_e60_stainjitter`, using `conf=0.05`
when review budget matters and `conf=0.01` when maximizing recall is more
important than false-positive load.
